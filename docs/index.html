What Kairos does

One wearable. A clear, calm picture of your day.

❤️‍🔥

Real-time stress tracking

Live stress index (🟢 Low / 🟡 Medium / 🔴 High) computed from HRV, sleep quality and movement synced from Fitbit.

⏰

Smart break reminders

Context-aware nudges when your stress index rises or you've been inactive too long.

🧘

Active break button

One tap: choose a full-body routine or a Targeted relief micro-video for your desk or workstation.

💪

Muscle-mass prevention

Integrates impedance body scales and strength-training tracking to counter sedentary muscle loss.

📈

Weekly reports

Weekly, monthly and yearly dashboards highlighting your most stressful days and trends.

🏢

Wellness dashboard (HR)

Employers see only aggregated, anonymous department averages — Logistics vs. Administration, and industry benchmarks.

📥 Input

  • Wearable (Fitbit API): heart rate & HRV, sleep hours, daily steps.
  • Manual profile: age, gender, height & weight, profession, department.
  • Optional: commuting method, weekly activity, medical notes.

⚙️ Processing

  • Stress index from HRV + sleep quality.
  • Sedentary-time detection & break logic.
  • Anonymisation & aggregation before any company view.

📤 Output

  • Employee: stress level, break alerts, personal reports.
  • HR: anonymous department dashboard & comparisons.

GDPR by design

Your individual data is never visible to HR.

  • 🔐 Health and stress data are anonymised and aggregated before any employer view.
  • 🏢 HR sees only group statistics (e.g. “IT Department”), never the individual.
  • 🤝 Employees choose whether to share data with HR — and can withdraw consent anytime from Settings → Privacy.
  • ⏳ Pseudonymised data is kept only as long as needed to compute monthly aggregates.

Future developments

Where Kairos is going next.

  1. Algorithm precision — improved stress-calculation accuracy.
  2. HR report — department-level averages, week-by-week trends, cross-department comparisons.
  3. Push notifications — timely, configurable nudges.
  4. Dedicated video database — no more reliance on YouTube links.
  5. Delegated authentication — “Sign in with Google” / corporate SSO.
  6. Stress map & company ranking — locate high-risk zones and reward well-being-first workplaces.
  7. Less obtrusive wearables — smart rings & bracelets instead of watches.
